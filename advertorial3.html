Cellulite, Simply Explained

Cellulite and sag aren’t a fat problem — they’re a connective-tissue problem. Fibrous bands under the skin (the septae) stiffen with age and pull the skin down; the fat between them pushes up. That push-and-pull is the dimpled, orange-peel look. This is why a product that only sits on the surface can never fix it — it has to reach the deep layer.

Scientifically Proven: The Right Active Works

Caffeine (guaraná) is one of the few cosmetic actives with real published research for the look of cellulite and firmness. In a 2025 study (Cosmetics, MDPI), a caffeine cream-gel massaged into the thighs and buttocks of 56 women significantly reduced the look of cellulite over 8 weeks; a 2026 randomized, double-blind trial (Pharmaceutics, MDPI) backed it up over 12 weeks. These studies evaluate the ingredient class, not one finished product. The massage matters — it’s how the active reaches the deep layer.
