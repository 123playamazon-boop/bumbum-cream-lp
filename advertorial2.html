Tip 1 — Clean Up What Feeds the Puffiness

It helps: cutting ultra-processed food, salt and sugar lowers water retention, so skin can look a touch less puffy and swollen.

But: it never touches the collapsed collagen bands under the skin — the actual cause of the dimples. You can eat perfectly and keep the exact same orange-peel texture. A support act, not the fix.

Tip 2 — Build the Muscle (Squats & Strength)

It helps: squats and lunges build the glute and improve your overall shape.

But: cellulite dimples sit on top of the muscle. You can build a firmer shelf and keep the same texture on top of it. Months of squats, zero change to the dimples — because you’re aiming under the problem, not at it.

Tip 3 — Boost Circulation (Dry Brushing, Massage, Coffee Scrubs)

It helps: brushing and massage push fluid and give a tighter look… for a few hours.

But: scrubs and lotions sit on the surface and wash off by your next shower. They never penetrate to the deep layer where the problem lives.

Tip 4 — Feed the Skin (Hydration, Collagen, Sleep)

It helps: water, collagen and good sleep genuinely support skin quality over time.

But: general skin support isn’t the same as reaching the septae — the fibrous bands that stiffen with age and pull the skin down into dimples. A good habit, not the fix.

So if tips 1–4 only get you halfway… what actually reaches the layer where the dimples form? Here’s the story a stranger told me at a pool party.
